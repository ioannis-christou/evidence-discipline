Over a research programme on financial and on-chain data, 72 pre-registered hypotheses were graded. Zero survived. The value was never the strategy — it was building instruments that make a false claim hard to state.

An analytics manager who can prove he kills his own good-looking results is worth more than one who reports them.

Computed Rather Than Assumed Costs

Most backtests must guess slippage, and the guess is often larger than the effect being measured.

On this venue price is a deterministic function of two reserves, so the fill can be computed exactly instead of estimated.

0.0000%
Error across 24 of 30 sampled real trades, reproduced to the base unit
6
Misses, which the code refuses to price rather than approximate

The 6 misses are a parameterisation the model does not cover — and the code refuses to price them rather than approximating. The refusal is the interesting part.

The Working Rules

  1. Pre-register the threshold before you look.
  2. Fail closed — absent is not zero. (Reading a missing field as zero once made correct arithmetic look 9.9% wrong and nearly got it thrown away.)
  3. Every claim needs a control that can fail: random entry, random pick, shuffled order, buy-and-hold, and a planted effect that must be detected.
  4. Refuse rather than approximate when the model does not cover the case.
  5. Check whether a quoted comment describes the present or the past.

Scale of the Test Harness

1,053
Automated checks, all green
29
Modules, each with a control that can fail

Every module carries a negative control (nothing passes on pure noise) and most carry a positive control (a deliberately planted effect IS detected).

Without a positive control, a null result cannot be distinguished from a broken test. That distinction is the difference between measuring reality and failing silently.

The transferable skill is not the venue. It is refusing to let a number through because it looks good.
